## 4. What the sublicense right is for

DohFlow's local application, its full feature set, and its AGPL-3.0 license
on the code are free forever — that promise is a standing product decision
(ADR 0066), not something this Agreement changes. The sublicense right in
§2 exists for one purpose beyond distributing the Project itself under
AGPL-3.0: it lets the maintainer build and offer **separate, optional paid
services around the free app** — a hosted sync/cloud tier being the current
example (ADR 0066) — under terms that may differ from AGPL-3.0, including
proprietary terms, without needing to track down and re-clear every prior
contributor individually before doing so.

**Project commitment.** The maintainer commits that Your Contribution, as
part of the Project's own public repository, stays licensed to the public
under AGPL-3.0-only. The sublicense right above is exercised only for
separate services built *around* the Project, never to make the public
repository itself, or a released version of it, retroactively
closed-source.

## Why this exists

The plain version, for anyone who does not want to read a legal agreement:
DohFlow's free, self-hosted app and its AGPL-3.0 license are permanent
(ADR 0066). This Agreement exists so the maintainer can also sell optional
services — like hosted sync — without one early contributor's code
accidentally blocking that later on. You keep authorship and copyright of
what you write; you're granting a license to use it, including in those
separate paid services, the same way most open-source projects with a
commercial arm ask contributors to.
