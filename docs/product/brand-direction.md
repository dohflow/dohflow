# Brand direction: DohFlow

- **Source:** owner feedback, 2026-09-01 (dogfooding PDF). Durable distillation;
  the working beads are the `twpo` wave, the `n76x` website epic, and the
  `hbg4f` licensing decision.
- **Status:** DECIDED and verified (2026-09-01). GitHub/npm/crates free;
  `dohflow.app` + `dohflow.io` free; `dohflow.com` squatted (offer optional).
  USPTO classes 9/36/42 clean. Guardrails: ceramics/talavera imagery, never
  squishy-compound-in-cans; no play+doh adjacency; no Simpsons framing;
  attorney clearance (incl. the DoughFlow payments platform sound-alike)
  before brand spend. Full risk register on `personal-cfo-n76x.1`.

## The name

**DohFlow** — cash flow, but cash became *dough* (the oldest slang for money),
and dough became *doh* to keep bread-making out of the search results. The
"doh" also winks at modeling clay — users sculpt their financial future with
the scenario tools — though **explicit Play-Doh references are off-limits in
imagery and copy** (owner's instruction; trademark caution). "Flow" carries
both the literal cash flow and the product's real differentiator: forward
planning, realistic scenarios of where cash is headed — not (primarily) budget
tracking and backwards looking, though it does that too.

## Visual identity

- Keep the existing palette core — the Mexican-flag green (`#006341`) and
  Saltillo terracotta already read as a pottery homage; expand with sparing
  accent colors only if genuinely needed.
- Target feel: the balance of **finance and fun**. A 3d-ish clay/pottery
  vibe — clay.com's whimsy is the tonal reference, but focused on finance
  rather than workflows.
- The scenario metaphor is the brand's centerpiece: **composing a scenario is
  sculpting; applying it is firing the kiln; the realized outcome is the
  pottery.**
- Website imagery: cartoonish clay-sculpting and talavera-style pottery as a
  visual language for discussing finances (an homage to Mexican pottery
  colors and forms).
- Financial numbers stay sober: whimsy lives in illustration, motion, and
  copy accents — never in the digits, tables, or forecasts.

## Website scope (feeds `n76x.4`/`n76x.6`)

Features pages; the **free** open-source app (with the honest caveat that the
optional SimpleFIN Bridge costs its users a little) vs the future cloud-hosted
paid plans; donation CTAs wherever sensible (buy-me-a-coffee style, mirrored
in-app for OSS users); a help center that grows over time (features, FAQs,
how it works); a **robust security page** detailing everything done to protect
user data; downloads (direct executable + GitHub, with contribute/submit-issue
paths); and a **blog/articles section** aimed at AEO/SEO — largely AI-drafted,
owner-reviewed posts about personal finance. An in-app feedback shortcut
(GitHub issue deep-link or AI-triaged email) is tracked as `1keh`.

## Launch (feeds `2ae0y`)

No paid marketing. Blog cadence for organic search, a ProductHunt launch,
Reddit posts where community rules allow — planned as a coordinated calendar
before launch day so the push lands together.

## Vision (recorded; acted on via `hbg4f` and the cloud-planning track)

- OSS launch first; **3–6 months** of driving traffic to the free version with
  a modest goal (~$100/month in donations) while feedback is gathered
  ruthlessly.
- In parallel, plan the **cloud-hosted subscription version** — the eventual
  income engine. The open question that blocks the public LICENSE swap is the
  licensing strategy (AGPL+DCO vs CLA vs source-available vs open-core):
  decision bead `hbg4f`, discussion with the owner in progress.
- Long-term: the best personal-finance tools in every format (desktop and
  mobile across OSes, MCP offerings inside Claude/ChatGPT), certified-planner
  tooling (a CFP works with clients through it, QuickBooks-accountant style),
  and eventually personalized financial-product offerings built on the user's
  own data — including a proprietary loan-aware "credit score." All
  non-OSS-tier ideas; the free self-hosted offering remains a fixed point.
